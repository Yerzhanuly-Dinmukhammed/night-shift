05 · the night shift

While you sleep, your body goes to work.

Brain cleaning

During sleep, fluid flushes waste out of the brain — including beta-amyloid, a protein linked to Alzheimer's.

space between brain cells: +60% in sleeping mice

Memory saving

Deep sleep and REM help move what you learned today into long-term memory. Cramming all night skips the "save" button.

sleep after studying = study that sticks

Body repair

Deep sleep is when the body recovers: muscles repair, energy is restored, and the immune system recharges.

deep sleep comes mostly early in the night

Mood & focus

REM sleep helps process emotions. After a bad night, stress feels bigger and focus gets weaker.

REM ≈ 25% of an adult's sleep
Xie et al., Science, 2013 · Sleep Foundation: Stages of Sleep
