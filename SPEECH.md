# The Night Shift — speaker script (3 speakers, ~8 min)

| Speaker | Slides | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–5: title, a third of your life, **live survey QR**, how much sleep, inside one night | ~2.5 min |
| 2 | 6–10: what sleep does, hours awake, **sleep debt simulator**, myths, bedtime calculator | ~2.5 min |
| 3 | 11–16: **live class results**, tips, coffee, **lark or owl test**, tonight's challenge, thank you | ~3 min |

The clock in the top-right corner moves from 22:00 to 07:00 — the whole talk is "one night".
Before the talk open `https://<your-app>.streamlit.app/?reset=nightshift` once to clear old answers.

---

## Speaker 1

**1. Title.** Good morning — or should I say good night. Our topic is sleep: why it matters and how to get better at it. We'll go through one night together, from 10 p.m. to 7 a.m.

**2. A third of your life.** You spend about one third of your life asleep. *(Drag the age slider.)* At 18 that's already six years. It's not lost time — it's when your brain does its most important work.

**3. Live survey.** Before we go on — phones out! Scan the code and answer three quick questions about last night. It's anonymous. *(Wait ~40 s; the counter goes up live.)* We'll see the results later.

**4. How much sleep.** Teenagers need 8 to 10 hours; adults at least 7. *(Drag the slider.)*

**5. Inside one night.** Sleep isn't "off". Every night we go through cycles of about 90 minutes: light sleep, deep sleep and REM, when we dream. Deep sleep comes early, REM near the morning.

## Speaker 2

**6. What sleep does.** While you sleep, your body goes to work: it cleans the brain — in a 2013 study the space between brain cells grew by 60% during sleep — saves memories, repairs the body and balances mood.

**7. Hours awake.** *(Drag to 17–19.)* After 17 to 19 hours awake, people performed like someone with 0.05% alcohol in their blood.

**8. Sleep debt.** One bad night is OK — but a whole week adds up. *(Click "exam week", then "catch-up weekend".)* Five nights of 6 hours and you're 10 hours behind. Sleeping 11 hours on Saturday and Sunday pays back only part of it — the battery is still not full.

**9. Myths.** *(Flip the cards.)* "I'll catch up at the weekend" — myth, you just saw why.

**10. Bedtime calculator.** Wake up between cycles, not in the middle. *(Press + / −.)* To get up at 7:30, fall asleep around 22:15.

## Speaker 3

**11. Live results.** Remember the survey? Here's this room. Our class slept on average ___ hours — the recommendation is 8–10. ___ of ___ slept less than 7 hours, and ___% used their phone in bed.

**12. Sleep tips.** So how do we fix it? Eight habits. *(Click the ones the audience already does — the score goes up.)*

**13. Coffee trap.** *(Move the slider.)* Caffeine's half-life is about five hours: a coffee at 4 p.m. is still about a third in your body at 11:30 p.m.

**14. Lark or owl?** Let's find out who you are — I need a volunteer! *(Five questions, the meter moves, the result appears. "Test someone else" for a second person.)*

**15. Tonight's challenge.** Pick just one thing and start tonight. *(Ask the audience, click their choice.)*

**16. Thank you.** It's 7 a.m., the sun is up. Thank you, and sleep well — questions?
